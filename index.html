About Meshona

Life doesn't come with a warning. Preparation changes everything.

Digital Nurse MeShona was created from a simple belief: taking care of yourself means more than understanding your health.

It means knowing your options. Protecting the people you love. Finding opportunities. Understanding your money. And preparing for the moments you never saw coming.

Through her work as a nurse, mother, and entrepreneur, Meshona brings healthcare knowledge and real-life perspective together to help people move through life with greater confidence and clarity.

— Meshona
